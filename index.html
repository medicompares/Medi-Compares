Frequently Asked Questions

Can I find cheaper alternatives for branded medicines?

Yes. We show you substitutes with the exact same composition, so you can pick a more affordable option without compromising on quality.

Do I need a prescription?

For most Rx medicines, yes — you'll need a valid prescription. You can upload it during checkout on the main site.

Is there any cost to use MediCompares?

No, comparing prices on MediCompares is completely free. You only pay the pharmacy for the medicine itself.

How often are prices updated?

Prices are refreshed regularly throughout the day so what you see is as close to real-time as possible.

Can I compare medicines by dosage and quantity?

Yes, you can filter results by dosage, pack size, and quantity to find the exact match you need.
